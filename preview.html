🥋 TAEKWONDO BELT SYSTEM

A Journey of Discipline to Mastery

9 Belt Levels. 1 Stronger You. Advance step-by-step through certified World Taekwondo (WT) Geup testing and character development.

🥋 Better Discipline
⚡ Improved Fitness
🎯 Stronger Focus
🏆 Confidence for Life
01

White Belt

10th Geup • Beginner

Signifies innocence and purity — a blank canvas ready to absorb foundational discipline, courtesy, and focus.

Focus: Basic Stances, Punches & Anti-Bullying Posture
02

Yellow Belt

9th Geup • Foundation

Signifies the earth where the plant takes root. The martial student begins building balance and body control.

Focus: Front Snap Kick (Ap Chagi), High/Low Blocks & Core Balance
03

Green Belt

8th Geup • Growth

Signifies the sprouting plant as Taekwondo skill begins to develop with rhythm, agility, and power.

Focus: Side Kick (Yeop Chagi), Taegeuk 1 & 2 Forms, Rapid Footwork
04

Green One (Green-1)

7th Geup • Intermediate

Transitioning toward higher technical precision, dynamic roundhouse combinations, and defensive parrying.

Focus: Roundhouse Kick (Dollyo Chagi), Taegeuk 3, Partner Sparring
05

Blue Belt

6th Geup • Rising Sky

Signifies the sky toward which the tree matures. Students demonstrate speed, tactical composure, and endurance.

Focus: Back Kick (Dwi Chagi), Taegeuk 4 & 5, Olympic Sparring Rules
06

Blue One (Blue-1)

5th Geup • Competence

Mastering combination strikes, feints, anti-grab self-defense evasions, and mental concentration.

Focus: Hook Kick, Taegeuk 6, Tournament Tactics & Joint Locks
07

Red Belt

4th Geup • Danger & Control

Signifies danger, cautioning the student to exercise strict self-control and warning opponents of lethal agility.

Focus: Spinning Hook Kick, Taegeuk 7, Advanced Kyorugi Strategy
08

Red One (Red-1)

3rd / 2nd Geup • Senior

Pre-Black Belt maturity. Refining power board breaking (Kyukpa), assistant teaching, and unwavering mental tenacity.

Focus: Taegeuk 8, Power Board Breaking, Full Contact Sparring
09

Black Belt 1st Dan / Poom

Kukkiwon / WT Certified

The pinnacle of martial mastery and character. Black is opposite to white, proving mastery over darkness and the true start of life leadership.

Mastery: Koryo Poomsae, National Athlete & Dojang Mentor

Children can begin Taekwondo training starting from 4 to 5 years of age. At early stages, training focuses on motor coordination, spatial balance, focus, listening habits, and playful martial fundamentals. We conduct separate, structured batches for Junior Kids (Ages 4-12) and Teens/Adults so that training intensity perfectly matches each student's developmental level.

Students learn emotional discipline and humility from their very first class. They are taught that martial skills must only ever be exercised for legitimate self-protection, fitness, and sanctioned sports tournaments—never for aggression or bullying.
Yes, absolutely! Taekwondo is an official Olympic medal sport governed worldwide by World Taekwondo (WT) and the Kukkiwon headquarters in Seoul, South Korea. Our academy’s curriculum strictly adheres to World Taekwondo regulations for Olympic Sparring (Kyorugi) and technical forms (Poomsae), qualifying students for official SGFI National School Games, CBSE Zonal Championships, and state and national tournaments.
Under our standardized 9-belt syllabus (White → Yellow → Green → Green-1 → Blue → Blue-1 → Red → Red-1 → Black Belt), rank examinations are conducted every 3 to 4 months based on regular attendance and technical mastery. With consistent training (3 to 5 sessions per week), an athlete typically earns their official 1st Dan / Poom Black Belt in 2.5 to 3.5 years.
